# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 29, 2026

Tend is a water-intake habit tracker. It does not collect, store, or transmit
any personal data.

- **No accounts.** There is no sign-in, and no user identifiers are created.
- **No network calls.** Tend has no backend and makes no network requests of
  any kind.
- **On-device storage only.** Your water log, daily goal, streaks, and garden
  are stored locally on your device using standard iOS local persistence.
  This data is never uploaded anywhere and is only accessible to you.
- **No third-party services.** Tend does not use analytics, advertising, or
  crash-reporting SDKs, and does not share data with any third party,
  because none is collected.

If you delete the app, all of its local data is deleted with it.
